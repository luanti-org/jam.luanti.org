### A little magic, a little game

You are to brew a small Luanti game, or a few. I recommend using an ingredient from the pantry, or two. Embrace rough edges, and share what you make. A simple idea is all it should take.

You have until **{{endsAt}}**. We are here to share novel experiences and mechanics with each other, so don't stress over it.

### Pack your spellbook and share your brew

Each entry must live in its own repository on a supported public git host, with these files at the root:

- `{{participationFile}}` with some indication that your game is taking part in the jam
- `game.conf` with your game's title and description
- Optional: `screenshot.png` to show off your game

The contents of `{{participationFile}}` are up to you, the file just needs to exist to count as a jam entry. I recommend including some interesting notes about your development journey!
